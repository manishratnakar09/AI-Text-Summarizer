Built to stay faithful to your text

  • Uses only information from your document — no outside facts.
  • Keeps names, dates and numbers; unsupported figures are rejected automatically.
  • Long documents are split into chunks so nothing is skipped.
  • If the AI service is unavailable, a local extractive summary is used instead.
  • API keys stay on the server — never in your browser.
