## 7. Observations & known limitations

- Extractive summaries are 100% faithful but can read slightly choppy because sentences are lifted unchanged; the hybrid/AI mode rewrites them into smoother prose.
- `long_document.txt` is the four samples repeated 10×; cross-chunk de-duplication removes the repeated sentences (the 'Repeat' column shows the effect).
- Scanned (image-only) PDFs contain no text layer, so they are rejected with a clear message (OCR is out of scope).
- PDF *download* uses a built-in Latin font; Hindi and other non-Latin text will not render in the PDF (TXT download works for all scripts).
- **Bugs found by this evaluation and fixed:** (1) duplicate sentences across chunks in very long documents (Repeat was 1.0), (2) symbols-only input was accepted, (3) text without punctuation came back unshortened.
- Stopword list is English-only; other languages still work but with less precise ranking.
